# How to Add Your Profile Image

## Steps:
1. Save your profile photo as `manish-profile.jpg`
2. Replace the existing file at: `client/public/images/manish-profile.jpg`
3. The image will automatically appear on your portfolio

## Image Requirements:
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: 400x400 pixels or larger (square aspect ratio recommended)
- **Quality**: High resolution for best results
- **Background**: Any background works (the design handles it beautifully)

## Current Setup:
- The portfolio is configured to use your image
- Fallback image is provided if your image doesn't load
- 3D effects and animations are already applied

Your portfolio now features a stunning 3D animated background!
