HOW TO ADD YOUR PROFILE PHOTO
==============================

Your portfolio is looking for: manish-profile.jpg

QUICK STEPS:
1. Save your professional photo (the one in black shirt)
2. Rename it to: manish-profile.jpg
3. Copy it to: client\public\images\manish-profile.jpg
4. Refresh your browser at localhost:3000

COMMAND TO COPY (after saving photo to Downloads):
copy "%USERPROFILE%\Downloads\manish-profile.jpg" "client\public\images\manish-profile.jpg"

The photo will appear in the pink circle above your name!
